How Redline holds together

One system at three intensities. The full study, with rules and rationale, is in the Stage 02.5 document.

Five working principles

FrameThe grid governs; images may break it by rule.
AnchorInformation sits where it is true, on the photo or the map.
RevealMotion uncovers facts inside a place. Nothing moves to look busy.
SyncList, map and image respond as one system.
SignalCrimson means selected, focused, here, or do this.

  • RevealAnchors emerge from the photo in reading order.
  • AnchorA pin or hotspot opens into its fact.
