# Al Aliah brand system (Stage 01, established)

Source of truth: `docs/00-master-brief.md`. Overrides: `docs/decisions.md`. If this file and the brief disagree, the brief plus the decision log win; fix this file.

## Position in one line
A modern **Abu Dhabi property advisory and discovery brand**, not a real-estate agency.
We help people *understand and navigate* Abu Dhabi property. We do not just "have properties".

## Essence and promise
- **Essence:** Clarity · Access · Confidence
- **Emotional outcome:** Clarity + Confidence (reduce uncertainty)
- **Archetype:** The Guide (primary), The Authority (secondary). Never "look how exclusive we are".
- **Pillars:** Knowledge · Access · Guidance · Momentum

## Personality calibration
Strongly modern · premium-accessible · restrained · authoritative but approachable · strongly advisory · sophisticated-practical.

## Naming
- Customer-facing: **AL ALIAH INTERNATIONAL**, or **AL ALIAH** where appropriate.
- Use the legal entity name only where legally required.

## Identity equity rules
- **Logo:** refine, don't reinvent. Keep the red architectural/skyline mark recognizable.
- **Color:** red is core equity. Evolve it toward a controlled *architectural crimson*, used deliberately and sparingly. Do **not** substitute black/gold, navy/gold, beige/gold or any generic UAE-luxury palette.
- Red reads as promotional or discounted when overused, and as confident and architectural when controlled. Treat overuse as a defect.
- Brand red must not double as the error/validation color (see `docs/open-questions.md` Q6).

## Voice
Concise, intelligent, confident, restrained, practical, knowledgeable, human.

Reject on sight:
- "Your trusted partner for all your real estate needs"
- "Leading innovative company committed to excellence"
- "Luxury living redefined"
- "Welcome to Al Aliah"
- any unsupported superlative ("best", "leading", "#1") without a cited source

Tone reference: "Abu Dhabi property, clearly understood." / "Local expertise for better property decisions."

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Brand campaign | Editorial, confident |
| Listing | Precise, information-rich |
| Investment | Analytical, evidence-based |
| Social | Fast, human, agent-led |
| Premium development | More aspirational |
| Contact / support | Friendly, direct |

## Place
Own **Abu Dhabi** (Abu Dhabi expertise with UAE reach). Abu Dhabi is the lead market for now; the final Abu Dhabi versus Dubai weighting depends on open-questions B1.
- **Never use Dubai imagery or landmarks for Abu Dhabi content.**
- Dubai may appear where contextually relevant: Dubai area pages, Dubai listings and projects, and UAE-wide content (D-032).
- Dubai content follows the same visual rules as everything else: real places and homes, no skyline-and-supercar luxury clichés, no gold or night-glamour treatments.

## Truthfulness rules (hard)
- Never invent metrics, transaction counts, awards, testimonials, licence numbers or client names.
- Never imply Al Aliah developed a project it brokers.
- Placeholder data in mockups must be visibly marked as placeholder.

## Not this brand
Generic luxury fantasy (gold, marble, yachts, jets, champagne) · Property Finder clone · property developer · traditional corporate site (stock photos, gradients, mission/vision blocks, icon grids, repetitive service cards).

## The ten-question check
Before approving any major decision: feels like Al Aliah? reinforces Abu Dhabi expertise? clearer? more confident? differentiating? works for premium *and* ordinary inventory? usable on mobile? scales to thousands of listings? works in Arabic? worth its performance cost? Any "no" means rethink it.
